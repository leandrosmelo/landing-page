YOUR DATA, CLEARLY EXPLAINED

Information about how Acceso ao Drive handles your Google account and Drive data.

01 About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Acceso ao Drive ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information — including Google user data — when you use our application. This policy is hosted on our official domain, melo.com.pt (https://melo.com.pt/privacy-policy/), and is linked from the App's home page and interface so you can find it easily.

02 Google user data we access

Acceso ao Drive integrates with Google Drive to provide its core functionality. With your explicit consent through Google's OAuth authorization flow, the App may access:

  • Your basic Google account profile information (name and email address) used to identify your account.
  • Metadata and content of files and folders in your Google Drive, only to the extent required to display, organize, or manage them within the App.

We only request the minimum Google API scopes necessary for the App to function, and access occurs only after you grant permission.

03 How we use Google user data

Google user data is used solely to provide and improve the App's user-facing features:

  • To authenticate you and connect the App to your Google Drive account.
  • To display and manage your Drive files and folders as requested by you within the App.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, selling to third parties, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.

04 Storage of Google user data

Acceso ao Drive does not permanently store your Google Drive file content on our servers. Authentication tokens are stored securely and used only to maintain your session. Any cached data is temporary and retained only as long as needed to provide the service.

05 Sharing of Google user data

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with third parties. Data is disclosed only:

  • To service providers strictly necessary to operate the App (e.g., secure hosting), under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

06 Your rights and controls

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account permissions page. Upon revocation, the App will no longer be able to access your Google data. You may also contact us to request deletion of any data we hold about you.

07 Changes to this policy

This policy is current as of the date shown above. If we change how Acceso ao Drive accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data, we will update this policy and notify users prominently within the App before the changes take effect. We encourage you to review this page regularly.

08 Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Acceso ao Drive handles your data, please contact us at privacy@melo.com.pt.
